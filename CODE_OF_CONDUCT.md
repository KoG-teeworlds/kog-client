# Code of conduct

Be respectful. Disagree with the work, not with the person. No harassment, no discrimination, no personal attacks,
no publishing other people's private information. Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits and issues that
break these rules and may ban people who do it repeatedly.

Report a problem with a person's behaviour to the maintainers privately (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for how).
