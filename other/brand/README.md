# KoG brand assets

Unmodified copies of the official King of Gores logos from
[KoG-teeworlds/brand_and_media](https://github.com/KoG-teeworlds/brand_and_media), commit
`700f64d29bca1413a0c1fbab435852a5cc2e47c7` (`brand/logo/` and `LICENSE`).

These files are **not** under the zlib licence of the client code. They are licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-NoDerivatives 4.0](LICENSE). Do not edit, recolour, crop or redraw them.
The KoG design team approved using them in the KoG client. Forks that are not KoG must replace them with their own.
The logo does not mean a build is an official KoG release.

Never copy files here by hand: `scripts/kog/sync_brand.sh` downloads them at the pinned commit and checks
`SHA256SUMS`; `scripts/kog/check_brand_assets.sh` (run in CI) fails if a file differs.
